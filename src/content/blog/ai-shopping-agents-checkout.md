# Your next customer might be an AI agent — and it is already checking out

For years, the job of a business website was to convince a person: good photos, clear prices, an easy checkout. That is still true. But on November 4, 2026, something changed in the background. Amazon opened a free self-service portal, the Shop Direct Merchant Workspace, that lets eligible US merchants connect their product feed to Amazon's search and to Alexa for Shopping — and, where a product is eligible, Amazon's AI can complete the purchase on the merchant's own website through a feature called Buy for Me. The customer may never see your home page at all.

Google moved the same day. Direct checkout in AI Mode and the Gemini app started rolling out to Canadian shoppers for eligible retailers, including select Shopify merchants. The shopper pays with Google Pay and never leaves the conversation.

## What actually changed

**Amazon's Shop Direct Merchant Workspace.** Shop Direct is Amazon's program for products from stores that do not sell in Amazon's own store. The new portal, announced November 4, 2026, is the self-service piece that was missing: a merchant registers, verifies the business website, and shares a product catalog through a feed partner or Amazon's Selling Partner API. There are no fees to take part, and merchants can opt out at any time. Shipping, returns and customer service stay with the merchant.

**Buy for Me.** This is the part that matters most. When a Shop Direct product is eligible, the shopper taps "Buy for Me", confirms the order details on Amazon, and Amazon's AI completes the purchase on the merchant's website. The order still arrives like any other order — from a buyer who may never have seen the store.

**Google's agent checkout.** In Canada, eligible retailers can now let shoppers check out directly inside AI Mode and the Gemini app. A simplified setup inside Google Merchant Center is promised for next year. Until then, the products that can be bought are the ones whose feed already holds up: accurate prices, real stock, clear titles, and pictures that show the product.

## The agents are already visiting your site

This is not a prediction about a distant future. Microsoft Clarity, a free site analytics tool, added an Agent Activity dashboard on November 4, 2026. It shows which AI agents visit a site and what they do once they arrive — searches, forms, logins, downloads, bookings and checkout. At launch it recognises agents run by Google, OpenAI and Anthropic. Microsoft's own analysis counted weekly sessions from AI agents nearly doubling in three weeks to almost 2 million, across 5.23 million agent sessions on sites using its tool between August 13 and September 9, 2026. That number counts only sites running Clarity, so treat it as a direction, not a market size — but the direction is clear.

Clarity also added a simulation: pick a task, an agent attempts it, and the report says whether it finished. The vendor's own warning is plain — an agent "might find the right product but miss a promotion, or reach checkout and get stuck," and "you may lose a customer without ever knowing why."

## What this means for your business

The practical lesson is simple, and it has nothing to do with hype. When an agent shops for your customer, your product feed is your storefront. The agent weighs a title, a description, a price and the product pictures. If the price is stale, the stock figure is wrong, or the only picture is a single white-background shot, the agent has less to go on — and the customer it represents may buy from a competitor whose feed is cleaner. The work that wins in this new channel is the same unglamorous work that always won: accurate prices, real stock levels, clear titles, and honest pictures of the product in use.

That also changes what a website has to do. A site that hides prices behind a "call for price" form, or whose checkout only works if a person reads a promotion banner, is a site an agent can fail on. And a failure is silent — there is no angry customer to tell you about it, just a lost sale you never saw. Running a free agent-activity check on your site, and a checkout simulation on your best-selling product, is now a sensible weekly habit, like checking your Google reviews.
